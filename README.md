## 📌 Ground Rules for Success

1. **Consistency beats intensity**: 1.5 – 2 hours every day > 10 hours on Sunday.
2. **Build while you learn**: Every topic is paired with hands-on code.
3. **DSA in parallel**: 3–4 problems a week alongside backend learning.
4. **Feasible & Necessary only**: Focus on what companies actually test in technical rounds.
